Privacy Policy
DONTCBOARD Privacy Commitments

1. Information We Store
When you publish a file, DONTCBOARD stores the document in Amazon S3 and stores the page name, file name, file type, file size, storage key, content type, and publication time in Amazon DynamoDB. The app also keeps a browser-local copy in IndexedDB for fallback access.

2. Live Screen Share Privacy
Live Screen Sharing is direct browser-to-browser peer-to-peer WebRTC streaming. No screen video or audio streams are recorded or stored on any server. Temporary signaling data is purged after the session ends.

3. AWS Services and Access
Document files are uploaded using temporary, signed AWS URLs. Viewer URLs are also temporary.

4. Your Responsibilities and Choices
Do not upload or share confidential or sensitive information unless you are authorized to do so.
